# Feature Dictionary

## Household_ID

Unique identifier for each household.

## Country

Country in which the household is located.

## City

City associated with the household.

## Family_Size

Number of people in the household.

## Num_Earners

Number of income earners in the household.

## Primary_Income

Income generated by the household's primary earner.

## Total_Household_Income

Combined household income across all earners.

## Estimated_Taxes

Estimated taxes associated with household income.

## Monthly_Expenses

Household monthly expenditure.

## Monthly_Savings

Amount saved by the household during the month.

Monthly savings may be negative when household expenditure exceeds available monthly financial resources.

## Net Income

Derived feature representing household income after estimated taxes.

## Savings Rate

Derived measure representing savings relative to household net income.

## Expense Ratio

Derived measure representing household expenses relative to net income.

## Income Per Capita

Total household income divided by family size.

## Income Per Earner

Total household income divided by number of earners.

## Financial Health Score

Derived measure combining household savings and expense behaviour.

## Health Category

Machine-learning target containing three categories:

- Poor
- Fair
- Good
