# Business Definitions

## Financial Health

Financial health represents a household's ability to manage income, expenses and savings.

In this project, household financial health is represented using derived financial indicators and classified into Poor, Fair or Good categories.

## Savings Rate

Savings rate measures the proportion of available household income that is saved.

Higher savings rates generally indicate stronger financial resilience.

## Expense Ratio

Expense ratio measures household expenses relative to available income.

A higher expense ratio means that a larger proportion of household income is consumed by expenses.

## Financially Vulnerable Household

A financially vulnerable household generally exhibits characteristics such as:

- Low savings
- High expense ratio
- Low income relative to household requirements
- Reduced ability to absorb unexpected expenses

## Income Per Capita

Income per capita represents household income available per family member.

It is calculated by dividing total household income by family size.

## Income Per Earner

Income per earner represents average household income attributable to each income earner.

## Financial Health Category

Households are grouped into three financial-health categories:

### Poor

Households showing relatively weak financial health.

### Fair

Households showing moderate financial health.

### Good

Households showing relatively strong financial health.
