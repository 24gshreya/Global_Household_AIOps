# Country and Regional Findings

## Household Income Distribution

Household income varies widely by country and region. Higher-income countries, including the USA, Germany, and the UK, show higher median household income than lower-income regions.

## Country Differences in Savings Rates

An ANOVA test found statistically significant differences in savings rates across countries with p < 0.05.
This indicates that country or region is a meaningful factor associated with household financial health.

## Relative Savings by Country

Germany, the UK, and the USA households save significantly less relative to other regions after controlling for income.

## Vulnerable Segments by Country

Certain countries are over-represented in financially distressed household clusters.
This pattern was identified using the country-cluster heatmap.
Larger family structures are also more frequently represented within financially vulnerable clusters.
